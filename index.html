Who We Are

ICW Ghana is a youth-led, impact-driven initiative dedicated to improving community health, education, and social well-being across Ghana. As part of the global ICW network, we collaborate with students, professionals, and partners to design sustainable projects that respond to real community needs.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower communities through inclusive healthcare outreach, education, advocacy, and volunteerism—creating measurable impact while nurturing the next generation of socially responsible leaders.

What We’re Working On

  • Community Health Outreach: Health education, screenings, and awareness campaigns.
  • Youth & Student Engagement: Training, leadership development, and volunteering opportunities.
  • Partnership Campaigns: Collaborations with schools, libraries, hospitals, and NGOs.
